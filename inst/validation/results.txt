Run date: 2026-11-19

Statistical tests

The complete testthat suite contains 19 test blocks: 12 for fitting/mapping
and seven for the existing branch-rate functions. All 633 assertions passed
with zero failures, test warnings or skips, including the installed-package run.

- ER maximum likelihood estimates were compared with independent scalar
  optimization of a likelihood calculated by exhaustive internal-state
  enumeration. Rate agreement tolerance: 1e-4; log-likelihood: 1e-7.
- ARD log likelihoods and marginal node probabilities were checked by exact
  enumeration for equal, stationary, FitzJohn and numeric root settings,
  including ambiguous and missing observations. Tolerances: 1e-6 and 1e-5.
- A three-state custom model was checked against exact enumeration; its
  shared rates and forbidden transitions were preserved.
- Root-state frequencies from 1,200 maps per root treatment were compared
  with exact conditional probabilities, using a six-standard-error bound
  plus 0.005 numerical allowance.
- In 2,000 symmetric binary bridge maps with known root state and q=0.7,
  change counts had the required parity. Their means agreed with the exact
  expectations q*tanh(q) and q/tanh(q), within six Monte Carlo standard errors.
- Tests checked taxon order, model selection, duplicate models, failure
  reporting, undefined AICc, reproducible parallel processing, caller RNG
  preservation, chunking, checkpoint extension and interrupted-run recovery.
- Original-map and stored-count inputs produced the same combined branch
  and marginal rate outputs. Forbidden transitions were absent from maps.

100-tip pilot

The reproducible script asr-smoke.R generates one 100-tip birth tree with
root age 10 and 20 independent binary characters. Both directional rates
are 0.1; root states have equal probability. Simulation uses independent
Poisson jump counts, not either inference engine.

Each character was fitted with ER and ARD using AICc, three random starts,
equal root probabilities and two workers. Mapping used 100 histories per
character, batches of 25, and stored branch counts on disk.

20/20 fits and 2,000/2,000 maps completed; no selected fit reached a bound.
ER selected: 18 characters; ARD selected: 2 characters.
Median selected q01: 0.1063989.
Median selected q10: 0.1079134.
Fitting elapsed time: 18.178 s; mapping elapsed time: 34.265 s.
Realized simulated changes over all characters/branches: 606.
Conditional mean reconstructed changes over all characters/branches: 666.8.

The per-character results are in parameter-estimates.csv and timing.csv.
This is a smoke test on one tree, not a general calibration study. Individual
estimated rates vary, and model selection did not always choose the generating
model. Conditional mapping intervals do not include Q, model or tree uncertainty.
More extensive simulation would be needed to establish repeated-sampling
coverage or performance under large state spaces and different sampling regimes.

Reproduce after installing the development package:
  Rscript inst/validation/asr-smoke.R /path/to/new-output-directory
